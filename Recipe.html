Dal Makhani

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Whole Black Lentils (Urad Dal) – rinsed and soaked overnight
  • ¼ cup Kidney Beans (Rajma) – rinsed and soaked overnight.
  • 3 tbsp Butter + 1 tbsp Ghee (or oil).
  • 1 Onion – finely chopped.
  • 1 Onion – finely chopped.
  • 1 cup Tomato Puree.
  • ¼ cup Heavy Cream.
  • Spices: 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp garam masala, and salt to taste.

Steps

  1. Boil: Pressure cook the soaked lentils and kidney beans in 4 cups of water with a pinch of salt until they are completely soft (about 20-30 minutes). Mash them slightly with the back of a spoon.
  2. Make the Base: In a separate pan, heat the ghee and 2 tablespoons of the butter. Sauté the onions until golden, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute.
  3. Simmer: Add the tomato puree, chili powder, and garam masala. Cook until the oil separates. Pour in the cooked lentils along with their cooking water. Simmer on low heat for 20-25 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Finish: Stir in the heavy cream and the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter. Simmer for 5 more minutes and serve hot!

Chole Bhature

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • Ginger-garlic paste, chole masala, cumin, salt
  • 1½ cups flour, 2 tbsp semolina, 2 tbsp yogurt (for bhature)
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Knead flour, semolina, yogurt, and a pinch of salt into a soft dough, then rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Pressure cook the chickpeas until soft.
  3. Sauté onion and ginger-garlic paste until golden, add tomatoes and spices, and cook until thick.
  4. Add the chickpeas with a little of their water and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Roll the dough into ovals and deep-fry until puffed and golden. Serve hot with the chole.

Dosa

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • Potatoes: 3 medium (boiled and roughly mashed)
  • Onion & Chili: 1 chopped onion, 1–2 green chilies
  • Spices: 0.5 tsp mustard seeds, 0.5 tsp turmeric powder, salt
  • Oil & Herb: 1 tbsp oil, a handful of coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Sauté aromatics: Heat oil in a pan. Splutter the mustard seeds, then fry the onions and green chilies until soft.
  2. Add seasoning: Stir in the turmeric powder and salt, cooking for 30 seconds
  3. Mix potatoes: Add the mashed potatoes and a splash of water. Mix well and simmer for 2 minutes until moist and cohesive.
  4. Finish: Garnish with coriander leaves and scoop it into your dosa.

Manchurian

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups grated cabbage and carrot
  • 3 tbsp flour + cornflour
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp chili sauce
  • Garlic, spring onion, oil

Steps

  1. Mix vegetables with flour and cornflour, shape into small balls, and deep-fry until golden.
  2. Sauté garlic in a separate pan, add soy sauce and chili sauce with a little water to make a thin gravy.
  3. Add the fried balls to the gravy and toss to coat.
  4. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.

Samosa

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour, 3 tbsp oil (for dough)
  • 3 potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • ½ cup peas, cumin, coriander, garam masala
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Knead flour with oil and water into a firm dough, rest 20 minutes.
  2. Cook peas and spices into the mashed potato to make the filling.
  3. Roll dough, cut into halves, shape into cones, and fill.
  4. Seal edges and deep-fry on low-medium heat until golden and crisp.

Paneer Tikka

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp tikka masala, ginger-garlic paste
  • Capsicum and onion chunks, oil

Steps

  1. Mix yogurt with tikka masala and ginger-garlic paste to make a marinade.
  2. Coat paneer, capsicum, and onion in the marinade, rest 30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill or pan-fry, turning occasionally, until lightly charred.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • ½ cup yogurt, 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, water as needed

Steps

  1. Knead flour, yogurt, oil, and salt into a soft dough. Rest 1 hour.

Chutney

Achar

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 raw mangoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • 1 tbsp mustard seeds, turmeric, chili powder, salt

Steps

  1. Toss diced mango with salt and turmeric, set aside 1 hour.
  2. Heat mustard oil, temper mustard seeds until they pop.
  3. Add chili powder and the mango, mix well.
  4. Store in a clean jar; flavors deepen after a day or two.

Gajar Halwa

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 cups grated carrot
  • 2 cups milk, ½ cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp ghee, cardamom powder, nuts

Steps

  1. Sauté grated carrot in ghee for 5 minutes.
  2. Add milk and simmer, stirring often, until it reduces and thickens.
  3. Stir in sugar and cardamom, cook until dry and glossy.
  4. Garnish with nuts and serve warm.

Milk Cake

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, cardamom powder

Steps

  1. Simmer milk, stirring often, until it reduces and thickens considerably.
  2. Add a few drops of lemon juice to help it granulate slightly.
  3. Stir in sugar and cardamom, keep cooking until it pulls away from the pan.
  4. Press into a greased tray, cool, then cut into squares.

Jalebi

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour, 2 tbsp yogurt (batter, rested overnight)
  • 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, few saffron strands (syrup)
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Boil sugar and water into a light syrup, add saffron, keep warm.
  2. Heat oil, pipe the batter into hot oil in overlapping spirals.
  3. Fry until golden and crisp on both sides.
  4. Dip immediately into the warm syrup for a minute, then remove.

Kheer

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup rice, rinsed
  • 1 litre milk, ½ cup sugar
  • Cardamom powder, nuts, saffron

Steps

  1. Simmer rice in milk on low heat, stirring often, until soft and the milk thickens.
  2. Add sugar and cardamom, cook 5 more minutes.
  3. Stir in saffron and nuts.
  4. Serve warm or chilled.

Rasgulla

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 litre milk, 2 tbsp lemon juice (for paneer)
  • 1 cup sugar, 4 cups water (for syrup)

Steps

  1. Curdle hot milk with lemon juice, strain and rinse the paneer, then knead until smooth.
  2. Shape into small smooth balls.
  3. Boil sugar and water into a light syrup.
  4. Add the balls to the boiling syrup, cover, and cook 15 minutes until they double in size.

Gulab Jamun

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder, 2 tbsp flour, pinch baking soda
  • 2 tbsp milk or cream, oil for frying
  • 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, cardamom (syrup)

Steps

  1. Mix milk powder, flour, and baking soda with milk into a soft dough. Shape into smooth balls.
  2. Boil sugar, water, and cardamom into a syrup, keep warm.
  3. Fry the balls on low heat, turning gently, until evenly deep brown.
  4. Soak in the warm syrup for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Mango Lassi

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 ripe mango, chopped (or ½ cup mango pulp)
  • 2 tbsp sugar, splash of milk

Steps

  1. Blend yogurt, mango, and sugar until smooth.

Chai

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water, 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp loose black tea
  • 2 tsp sugar, crushed cardamom and ginger

Steps

  1. Boil water with ginger and cardamom for 2 minutes.
  2. Add tea leaves, simmer 2 minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar, bring to a boil, then simmer until deep in color.
  4. Strain into cups and serve hot.

Aam Panna

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 raw green mangoes
  • ½ cup sugar or jaggery
  • Roasted cumin, black salt, mint leaves

Steps

  1. Boil raw mangoes until soft, then scoop out the pulp.
  2. Blend pulp with sugar, cumin, black salt, and mint.
  3. Dilute with cold water to a drinkable consistency.
  4. Chill and serve over ice.

Masala Chaas

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yogurt, whisked
  • 1½ cups cold water
  • Roasted cumin powder, black salt, chopped coriander
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Whisk yogurt with water until smooth and frothy.
