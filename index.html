Our Favourites

Chole Bhature is a celebrated North Indian delicacy, elevated through rich spices, slow cooked chickpeas, and perfectly crafted bhature that are light, golden, and irresistibly soft. The dish blends depth, warmth, and tradition, offering a luxurious balance of bold flavours and refined textures. Often enjoyed as a signature meal, Chole Bhature represents the richness of Indian cuisine — comforting, indulgent, and timeless.

Samosas are a refined Indian classic — crisp, golden pastry filled with delicately spiced potatoes, peas, and aromatics. Each piece offers a perfect balance of texture and flavour, combining warmth, richness, and tradition. Paired with smooth mint or tamarind chutney, samosas bring an elegant touch to Indian cuisine, crafted with care and served with pride.

Gulab Jamun is a signature Indian dessert crafted from soft, milk‑rich dumplings soaked in warm saffron‑rose syrup. Served in elegant bowls, each piece carries a glossy golden finish and a melt‑in‑your‑mouth texture that feels indulgent and refined. With its rich aroma and smooth sweetness, Gulab Jamun brings a luxurious, timeless charm to any dining experience.

Lassi is a signature Indian drink crafted from smooth, cultured yogurt blended with saffron, rose, or fresh mango. Served chilled in elegant glasses or bowls, it delivers a creamy, refreshing richness that balances spice with sweetness. Its silky texture and aromatic notes make Lassi a luxurious companion to any premium Indian meal — refined, cooling, and timeless.
